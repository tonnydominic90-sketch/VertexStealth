Multi-profile management for Chrome and Firefox

Multi-profile management means running several separate browser profiles side by side, each with its own cookies, proxy and settings, and keeping them organized in one place. VertexStealth is a Windows multi-profile manager for Chrome and Firefox.

What is multi-profile management?

Most people end up with different browsing contexts: a test environment, a client workspace, a research session. Mixing them in one browser means shared cookies, logins and settings. A multi-profile manager gives every context its own profile and one dashboard to create, launch and maintain them.

What can you do with several profiles?

  • QA and web testing: reproduce different locales, timezones and device presets without touching your own browser.
  • Digital marketing: keep client or campaign workspaces separate.
  • Research and analysis: run separate sessions with their own proxies and saved state.
  • Developer workflows: use each profile's local automation endpoint where the running browser supports it.

How does VertexStealth organize your profiles?

  • One overview table lists every profile with launch, edit, duplicate and delete actions.
  • Running profiles appear first, and proxy, location and WebRTC status are visible per profile.
  • Duplicate a profile to reuse its settings, or start from a desktop or mobile preset, or randomize supported settings.
  • Cookie and session tools work per profile.
  • Your profile list is backed up in rolling snapshots that you can restore from Settings.

Where is my profile data stored?

On your computer. VertexStealth does not host browser sessions in the cloud and does not share profiles between accounts. To enforce plan limits it keeps an opaque identifier for each profile with your account; see the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I manage multiple profiles in VertexStealth?

  1. Create your first profile. Choose Chrome or Firefox, name the profile and set its proxy and browser settings.
  2. Duplicate for related profiles. Duplicate a profile to reuse its settings, then change only what differs.
  3. Launch and verify. Launch the profiles you need and run Fingerprint Check on each.

Responsible use

Using several profiles or accounts can be restricted by the terms of the platforms you use. VertexStealth is a general-purpose tool: follow applicable laws and each platform's rules.
